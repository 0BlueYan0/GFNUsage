# Changelog

[繁體中文](CHANGELOG.zh-TW.md)

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-22

### Added

- The main panel charts the period: how the quota has run down, and where it is
  heading. Hover the chart for the month-end figures.
- The progress bar marks where your pace should be. Hover the bar for the
  figures behind it.

### Changed

- The forecast follows the last seven days of play instead of averaging the
  whole period.

## [0.1.3] - 2026-09-22

### Fixed

- The panel updates its numbers when it opens.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-09-22

### Fixed

- Checking for updates times out after 12 seconds, and retries without the
  system proxy when that fails.
- Checking for updates no longer disables the buttons on the main panel.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-09-22

### Fixed

- Checking for updates reports the result: already up to date, or why it could
  not check.
- Installing an update on macOS completes and restarts the app.
- The update banner's text lines up with its buttons.
- The settings page keeps its header in view while the list scrolls.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-21

### Added

- Remaining monthly hours in the Windows system tray and the macOS menu bar.
- A pace threshold and an overrun projection measured against playable time.
- How much you can still play today, and the hours you are on track to leave
  unused.
- Recent play sessions: each game, when, and for how long.
- Blackout windows, with export and import.
- Sign-in with an NVIDIA account, renewed in the background.
- An update check, with the install one click away.
- Start with Windows.
